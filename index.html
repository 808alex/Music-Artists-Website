My Top 10 Favourite Artists

Osamason

Osamason

Osamason is probably my favourite artists because of his versatility, he can hop on a calm rage beat or a over the top heavy 808 distorted beat and walk the floor. His flows are always good and it seems like he doesn't even try but still makes some of the best music.

Friendly Thug 52

Friendly Thug 52

Friendly Thug hits deep, finding out about him through a person I used to be really close with, he is one of the first Russian rappers that really hit home for me, I speak Russian with all of my family so it was instantly a fresh air whilst understanding him. Friendly Thug is very rooted in his people and beliefs and doesn't switch up for money, he's probably the realest rawest Russian rapper.

Nettspend

Nettspend

Nettspend was the first rapper I listened to more than any mainstream artist, I knew nettspend before he even had 100k monthly listeners making snippets in his bathroom. Although his recent music is very hit or miss for me, his cash era was insane and really molded my music taste.

Madk1d

Madk1d

Madk1d is another Russian rapper but this time is completely different, his vocals are extremely unique and distinctive, he mixes very harsh distorted vocals onto club electronic distorted beats, his lyrics are also very unique usually rapping about problems and topics but in different lights.

Villian

Villian

Villian hopped onto the scene out of nowhere, as he famously says in Russian "we sprinted the whole industry", he literally spedran his way to the top with his clean vocals on very dark jumpy beats.

Untiljapan

Untiljapan

Untiljapan is the best lyrical underground artist by far, his music helped me through a lot of hard times. He mixes heartfelt lyrics onto atmospheric late night clams casino type beats and just hits everytime. People call him a poet and not a rapper.

Protect

Protect is what you would call high music, it's just ballhog, you either get it or don't, he just hops on the mic and says whatever and everything flows so nicely. He's also the best dressed artist in the scene.

Matt Proxy

Matt Proxy

Matt Proxy is probably the closest thing we will get to prime Kanye, his production skills, mixing so many genres but still keeping to his sound, nice distorted mixing, he's just a genius, he also recorded his whole way, he used to record youtube tutorials showcasing his sauce and that's the sauce that got him to where he is now.

Fakemink

Lucki
